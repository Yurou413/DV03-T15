Hospitalised Injury Insights: First Nations Australians

Hospitalised injuries represent a substantial public health challenge in Australia. This project presents an in-depth exploratory analysis of injury admissions, disparities, and health outcomes between First Nations and non-Indigenous populations across an 11-year observation window (2011–2021).

  • Longitudinal Disparities: First Nations people face disproportionately higher rates of injury hospitalisation compared to non-Indigenous Australians throughout the decade.
  • High-Risk Mechanisms: Transport incidents, road users, and external causes reveal distinct risk profiles across demographic cohorts.
  • Age & Regional Diversity: Vulnerability shifts significantly across age brackets and geographic remoteness, highlighting the need for targeted health interventions.
  • Jurisdictional Differences: Variations across Australian states and territories demonstrate diverse regional healthcare dynamics and net changes over time.
